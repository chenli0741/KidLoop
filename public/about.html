KidLoop helps parents, authorized drivers and transportation coordinators plan and complete school and after-school rides.

Sign in · Privacy policy · Support
KidLoop is an account-based transportation service for adults. Participating schools, after-school programs and transportation organizations provide accounts and control access to their operational records.

What KidLoop does

Transportation teams use KidLoop to maintain school calendars, riders, drivers, vehicles and routes. Drivers use the app to view assigned rides and record pickup and drop-off status. Parents can view the schedules and records available for their linked children.

Optional Google Calendar connection

A driver may connect a Google account to place assigned KidLoop rides on that account's primary calendar. KidLoop requests permission to identify the selected Google account and create, update or remove only the calendar events that KidLoop manages. It does not request access to Gmail or unrelated calendar data. The connection is optional and can be disconnected from KidLoop.

Google API data use

Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide the requested Gmail sending or driver calendar synchronization features. It is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train AI models. KidLoop does not use Google APIs to create, distribute or facilitate AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery. Our handling of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
