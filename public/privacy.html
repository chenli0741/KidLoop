Kid Loop Rides

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

School pickups. Clear schedules. Connected families.

Kid Loop Rides (also called Kid Loop) helps parents, drivers and transportation coordinators manage children's school and after-school journeys. This policy explains the information handled by the service and how to request help with your data.

1. Scope and account access

Kid Loop Rides is operated by Li Chen. For privacy questions or requests, email chenli0741@gmail.com.

Kid Loop is an account-based service used by adults. Accounts are provided by a participating school, after-school program or transportation organization. That organization manages its operational records and determines who is authorized to access them. This policy covers the Kid Loop app and website; the organization may also provide its own privacy notice.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: names, email addresses, telephone numbers, account identifiers, roles and links between parents and children. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, rather than readable passwords.
  • Student information: names, ages, grades, classrooms, schools, after-school programs, family contacts, optional student photos and notes supplied by authorized users.
  • Transportation records: pickup and drop-off addresses, schedules, routes, driver and vehicle details, attendance, absence requests, parent instructions, pickup and arrival times, and status-change history.
  • Technical information: session identifiers, language and login preferences, and information processed when connecting to the service, such as IP addresses, browser or device information and server error logs.

Information comes from account holders, authorized organizational staff and use of the service. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information in free-text notes.

3. How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, show the correct child's schedule, organize transportation, identify students at handoff, communicate daily instructions, record ride status, maintain operational history, respond to requests and protect the service from misuse.

Student photos are used for visual identification by authorized people. The current app does not perform facial recognition. Address-based maps display pickup and drop-off locations; the current app does not continuously track a child's or driver's GPS location.

4. Who can access information

Information is available to authorized organizational administrators, assigned drivers and linked parents according to their roles. Parents can access the children explicitly linked to their accounts. Assigned drivers can access the information relevant to their trips.

Hosting, database and file-storage providers process information needed to operate the service. Kid Loop uses Vercel hosting and private photo storage, along with a hosted PostgreSQL database. When a map or linked resource is loaded, its provider, such as Google Maps, may receive the requested address and technical connection information and apply its own privacy terms.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to investigate misuse, protect safety or respond to a valid legal request. The current app does not include advertising or advertising-tracking SDKs.

5. Photos and device permissions

When you choose to add a student photo, the app may request camera or photo access. Only the photo you select or capture for upload is sent to the service. You can deny or revoke permission in your device settings and continue using features that do not require a photo. Revoking device permission does not delete a previously uploaded image.

6. Cookies and session preferences

Kid Loop uses cookies to maintain sign-in sessions and language preferences. If you choose “remember me,” a session can last up to 30 days; otherwise the server session lasts no more than 12 hours. A saved email hint may remain for up to 180 days. Signing out revokes the current session. The app does not store your readable password in cookies or local storage.

7. Retention and security

Account and transportation records are retained while needed to provide the service and maintain the organization's operational history, including applicable recordkeeping and dispute-resolution needs. Archiving or deactivating a record may hide it from normal use without deleting it. Kid Loop does not apply a single automatic deletion period to all student and transportation records.

Deletion requests are reviewed with the organization responsible for the records. Some records may need to be retained for legal or operational reasons, and backup copies may remain until they are replaced through the provider's backup cycle. We use role-based access controls, password hashing and protected sessions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

Privacy contact: Li Chen — chenli0741@gmail.com. You may email this address directly to request access, correction, photo removal, account deletion, deletion of a child's records, or withdrawal of consent. We may need to verify your identity and coordinate with the organization responsible for the records before completing the request.

You can update information available in your account and, as a linked parent, supported fields for your children. To request access, correction, removal of a photo, account deletion, deletion of a child's records, or withdrawal of permission for future processing, contact the school, after-school program or transportation organization that provided your Kid Loop account using its established contact channel.

Ask for the administrator responsible for Kid Loop accounts and state that your request concerns “Kid Loop Rides privacy.” The administrator can verify your relationship to the account or child and coordinate the request with the service operator. Provide only the details needed to identify the relevant account; never send your password. Removing information needed for transportation may prevent continued use of that service.

9. Children's information

Kid Loop is intended for parents, authorized drivers and staff, not for children to create independent accounts. Children's information is supplied and managed by parents, guardians or authorized organizations for transportation coordination. If you believe a child's information has been entered without appropriate authorization, contact the responsible organization through the process above so the information can be reviewed and addressed.

10. Processing locations and updates

Information may be processed in the countries where Kid Loop's service providers operate, which may differ from your location. This policy may be updated when features or practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be described in an updated notice.
