## 简体中文

副标题：学校与课外班接送协同助手

宣传文本：集中查看孩子的接送安排、司机车辆和上车送达状态。家长可提前请假或留言，司机按行程处理接送，帮助日常交接更清晰。

描述：

KidLoop 帮助家长、司机和接送管理人员共同安排孩子往返学校、课外班及其他接送地点的日常行程。

家长可查看已绑定孩子的每日安排、司机和车辆信息，以及实际上车和送达时间；提前提交请假或特殊要求，让接送人员及时了解当天变化。

司机可查看分配给自己的行程和学生名单，记录已接到、已送达或缺席状态，并查看相关家长留言。

管理人员可维护学生、学校、课外班、车辆及司机资料，安排接送线路和每日行程，查看处理进度及状态历史。

支持简体中文和英文界面。

使用 KidLoop 需要由服务组织管理员提供账号。不同角色只能访问获授权的信息；家长只能查看与账号绑定的孩子。

关键词：接送,校车,学校,课外班,家长,学生,司机,行程,排班,儿童接送,放学,出行

## English

Subtitle: School & After-School Rides

Promotional text: Keep school pickups organized. View schedules, driver and vehicle details, and pickup status. Share absences and notes with your child's transportation team.

Description:

KidLoop helps parents, drivers, and transportation coordinators organize children's daily trips between schools, after-school programs, and other pickup and drop-off locations.

Parents can view schedules for their linked children, driver and vehicle details, and recorded pickup and arrival times. Submit an absence in advance or leave a note about special requests.

Drivers can view assigned trips and student rosters, record pickups, drop-offs, or absences, and read relevant parent notes.

Coordinators can manage students, schools, after-school programs, vehicles, and drivers; arrange routes and daily trips; and review trip progress and status history.

Available in English and Simplified Chinese.

A user account provided by your service organization's administrator is required. Access depends on the assigned role. Parents can only view children linked to their account.

Keywords: school,pickup,dropoff,after school,family,rides,transport,driver,schedule,students,carpool
